About Fitora

Making getting dressed fun again.

Fitora started with a simple question: why is it so hard to decide what to wear, when your closet is full of clothes? We're building an app that makes getting dressed fun again: a place to share your style, get inspired by others and actually use the clothes you already own.

Fitora is a product of Heebond, a small Dutch company from Lichtenvoorde. We design and build the app ourselves, with a focus on a friendly community, honest technology and respect for your privacy.

What we stand for

A safe community. New posts and comments are checked before they appear, and you can report or block anyone at any time.

Honest about AI. We tell you when AI is used and what it does, and we never pretend something works when it doesn't.

Clear about money. Sponsored brands and affiliate links are always labelled, so you know exactly what you're looking at.
