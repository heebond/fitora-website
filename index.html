Getting dressed, made fun again.

Fitora turns the clothes you already own into outfits you actually want to wear.

1

Your whole closet, in your pocket.

Snap a photo of a piece you own, or add it straight from the Fitora Shop. Fitora fills in the type, colour and brand for you, so your closet stays organised without the effort.

2

See it on before you wear it.

Mix and match your pieces, or tap Build My Fit for a suggestion. Then tap Try on and see the outfit on your own mannequin, worn the way it would really look.

3

Plan your week in seconds.

Love a look? Save it, share it with friends in the chat, or plan it for any day of the week. No more standing in front of your closet in the morning.

Questions?

When can I download Fitora?

Fitora is in testing right now and is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

Is Fitora free?

Yes. Sharing outfits, chatting, your closet and planning outfits are free. Every month you also get free AI try-ons. Later you'll be able to buy extra try-ons in the app.

How do the shop links work?

Under some outfit posts Fitora shows matching products from online shops. These are affiliate links: if you buy something through them, Fitora may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Brands that pay for a place in the Fitora Shop are always labelled "Sponsored".

Is my data safe?

Your closet photos are private and only visible to you, unless you share an outfit yourself. You can delete your account and all your data at any time in the app. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

Who is behind Fitora?

Fitora is made by Heebond, a small company from Lichtenvoorde, the Netherlands. More about us.
