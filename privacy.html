Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Fitora app collects, why, who we share it with and what your rights are. Fitora is a product of Heebond (Groenling 24, 7132 EE Lichtenvoorde, the Netherlands, KvK 94831203), which is responsible for your data. Questions? Email service@heebond.info.

1. What we collect

Account: your email address, username, display name and, if you add them, a profile photo and bio. When you sign up, we record that you confirmed you are 16 or older and that you accepted our Terms of Use.

What you share: posts (a photo and caption), comments, likes, saved posts, who you follow, and messages you send in the chat.

Your closet: photos of your clothes and their details (type, colour, brand, size), your outfits, your outfit planner and the mannequin you chose. Closet photos are private: only you can see them, unless you share an outfit in the chat yourself.

Shop clicks: when you tap "Shop" on a product, we count the click anonymously (only the product and the time, not who clicked), so we can show brands how many visitors they receive.

We do not show you third-party advertising and we do not sell your data.

2. AI features

Some features use AI services. Before you use an AI feature for the first time, the app explains what is sent and asks for your permission.

Claude (Anthropic): checks new posts and comments for inappropriate content before they appear; recognises the type, colour and brand of a clothing photo you add; and suggests outfits with Build My Fit (for this, only a text list of your clothes is sent, no photos).

Google (image AI): when you tap Try on, the photos and names of the pieces you picked are sent to Google to create a picture of your mannequin wearing the outfit.

These services only use your data to perform the task and do not use it to train their AI models. The results (for example a try-on picture) are stored in your Fitora account.

3. Why we use your data

We use your data to provide the app and its features (performance of our agreement with you), to keep the community safe by checking content and handling reports (our legitimate interest and legal obligations), and for AI features you choose to use (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

4. Who we share data with

We use carefully selected service providers that process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, login and file storage), Resend (sending account emails such as login codes), Anthropic (Claude) and Google (try-on images), and Apple (if you buy extra try-ons in the app, in the future). Other users only see what you share publicly or send to them. If your profile is private, your posts are only visible to followers you approve.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union. In that case we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account in the app (Profile → Settings → Delete account), your profile, photos, closet, outfits, posts and messages are deleted permanently. Reports about content may be kept for a limited time where needed to keep the community safe.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, to receive a copy of your data and to withdraw your consent. Email service@heebond.info and we'll reply within one month. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

7. Age

Fitora is only for people aged 16 and older.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. We'll show the date of the latest version at the top, and tell you in the app about important changes.
